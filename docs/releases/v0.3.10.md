## The pane card announces its colour

Every pane is a rounded card: the card and its padding are painted the
host's panel colour, while the grid inside belongs to the app. A
full-screen TUI that fills every cell in its own colour therefore left a
ring of host background between its page and the card edge, so the app
looked like it had carved out a smaller area than the terminal gave it.

A spawned pane now carries `MTTY_TERM_BG` in its environment: the
`#rrggbb` colour of that card, padding included. An app that paints its
page in that colour sits flush in the card with no ring. Apps that ignore
the variable render exactly as before, so nothing changes until an agent
also adopts it.

## Maintenance and verification

Cross-platform CI covers Linux, macOS and Windows, along with rendering and
release-mode performance gates.

Packages: macOS ARM64 and Intel ZIP bundles; Linux x86-64 tar.gz, AppImage and
deb; Windows x86-64 ZIP and MSI. Existing macOS signing and notarization remain
in place.
